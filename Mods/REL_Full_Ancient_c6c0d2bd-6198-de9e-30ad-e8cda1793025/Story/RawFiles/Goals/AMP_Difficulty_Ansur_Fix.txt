// to dragon form - that combo wiped the SPENT marker, so undying will kept

// so vanilla downed wins and he dies on the first KO. re-applied on a couple
// later events in case polymorph/scene swap strips it.

Resurrected(S_WYR_SkeletalDragon_67770922-5e0a-40c5-b3f0-67e8eb50493a)

AND
