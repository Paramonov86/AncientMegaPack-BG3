// Ka'vir the Silver - githyanki stunner (ULTRA pool #8).

USING rogue_smart
// inherits: HIT_CHANCE_STUPIDITY ~0.85, +KILL_ENEMY, +CONCENTRATION_REMOVE 0.30, flanking/endpos-neighbour zeroed.

// --- ENGINEERED MELEE HIT-AND-RUN (no vanilla precedent: only ranged use negative endpos) ---
// endpos penalty is scored on the FINAL tile only, not the momentary approach for the swing
// (and he ignores AoO), so adjacency-to-attack is free. The trick is the MULTIPLIER x MAX_DISTANCE
// pairing: a strong peel-pressure (-0.50, ranged-tier) PLUS a SMALL safe radius (7m) that is
// reachable right after the swing with his huge move pool -> close, hit, dance out to 7m, repeat.
// Failure modes proven in testing: -1.50 + 22m = safe zone unreachable while attacking -> pure flee,
// never hits; -0.10 + 6m = penalty too shallow to spend movement -> stands glued, no flex.
MULTIPLIER_ENDPOS_ENEMIES_NEARBY                -0.35
ENDPOS_ENEMIES_NEARBY_MIN_DISTANCE               1.90
ENDPOS_ENEMIES_NEARBY_MAX_DISTANCE               7.00

// --- IGNORE opportunity attacks entirely (Scout darts through the line) ---
ENABLE_MOVEMENT_AVOID_AOO                        0.00
MULTIPLIER_BOOST_IGNORE_AOO                      0.00

// --- JUMP CONSTANTLY, even short hops ---
SPELL_JUMP_MINIMUM_DISTANCE                      1.50
SPELL_TELEPORT_MINIMUM_DISTANCE                  1.50
FALLBACK_JUMP_OVER_WALK_PREFERRED_DISTANCE       0.50

// --- RECKLESS (Mad Max): ignores self-damage and fall damage ---

MULTIPLIER_FALL_DAMAGE_SELF                      0.00
MULTIPLIER_REFLECT_DAMAGE                        0.00
